Honesty first. DOT: a checked-bag fee refund is due when the bag is lost or significantly delayed — domestic 12 hours after you had a chance to deplane; international 15 hours if the flight was 12 hours or less, 30 hours if longer — and you filed a Mishandled Baggage Report (MBR). The refund is automatic once both are true. This card does the threshold math from your times. It does not file an MBR, log into an airline, or start a claim. Sources: DOT Refunds · 14 CFR §260.5. When is a checked bag “significantly delayed” for a fee refund?

Domestic 12h

DOT: the bag is significantly delayed if it is not delivered within 12 hours of the opportunity to deplane at the final destination (US domestic).

International 15h / 30h

International: 15 hours if that flight segment was 12 hours or less; 30 hours if the flight was more than 12 hours.

MBR is required

14 CFR §260.5: the carrier refunds the checked-bag fee when the passenger files a Mishandled Baggage Report. Automatic once the delay is significant and the MBR is on file. This tool does not file it.
